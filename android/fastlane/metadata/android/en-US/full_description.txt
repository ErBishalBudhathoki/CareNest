CareNest brings the entire NDIS care operation into one place — scheduling, service delivery, invoicing and claiming — so providers spend less time on administration and more time on care.

Built for Australian NDIS providers, support coordinators, plan managers, support workers and the participants and families they serve.

ONE CLEAR VIEW OF YOUR BUSINESS
• A live business overview of active clients, staff, invoices and requests
• Manage multiple organisations with organisation-scoped data
• Role-based access, so admins, workers, participants and families each see what they need
• A dedicated Configuration & Finance area for the financial tools only administrators should use

SCHEDULE CARE WITH CONFIDENCE
• Assign shifts to the right support worker and confirm the booking
• Recurring schedules, assignment details and shift notes
• Real-time schedule updates and push notifications
• Geofenced clock-in reminders — never silent location tracking

NDIS-READY BILLING, END TO END
• Generate client and employee invoices from actual worked time
• NDIS support-item pricing with price caps, region rules and price-guide support
• Review every line against the current NDIS cap before it is billed
• SIL Rosters of Care, SIL invoice generation and NDIS claiming in dedicated admin workflows
• myplace-ready bulk payment request (BPR) exports
• Standard invoicing and SIL claiming are kept separate, so the same hours are never billed twice
• Bulk invoice generation, PDF invoices, email delivery and payment records
• Idempotent generation: a retry never produces a duplicate invoice or claim batch

A WORKSPACE YOUR TEAM WILL ACTUALLY USE
• Clock in and out, with timesheets, weekly targets and daily breakdowns
• Leave insights, shift history and your own payslips
• Mileage and expense tracking with reports
• Training and compliance records with expiry tracking
• Secure messaging between workers, participants and families
• An offline queue that syncs when connectivity returns
• A hands-free voice assistant for common navigation
• Pay rates, certification requirements and team coordination tools

FOR PARTICIPANTS AND FAMILIES
• A dedicated client portal with appointments and service history
• View, approve or dispute invoices
• Family access with a clear, granular permission matrix
• Secure messaging and feedback, without exposing other participants' data
