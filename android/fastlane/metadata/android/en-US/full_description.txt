CareNest is the operating system for NDIS care providers. Plan the roster, deliver the shift, invoice the hours and lodge the claim — without chasing spreadsheets, screenshots or paper timesheets.

Built for Australian disability support providers, support coordinators, plan managers, support workers, and the participants and families they support.

SEE THE WHOLE BUSINESS
One live dashboard shows active clients, staff, shifts, invoices and requests. Switch between organisations without ever mixing their data, and give every role — admin, worker, participant, family — exactly the screens they need. Financial tools live in a dedicated Configuration & Finance area that only administrators can reach.

BOOK CARE IN SECONDS
Assign a shift, add recurring schedules and shift notes, and let staff see their whole day on their phone. Clock in and out with geofenced reminders (never silent location tracking), then review timesheets, weekly targets, overtime and shift history. Leave balances, expense reports, mileage and payslips live in the same workspace.

BILL NDIS, CORRECTLY
Generate invoices from actual worked time and check every line against current NDIS support-item price caps, region rules and the price guide. Standard invoicing and SIL claiming stay strictly separate, so the same hours can never be billed twice. Produce PDFs in bulk, email them, record payments, and retry safely — idempotent generation prevents duplicate invoices or claim batches.

SIL, SORTED
Manage Supported Independent Living in dedicated workflows: properties, quotes, Rosters of Care, rostering, SIL invoice generation, invoice dispatch and NDIS claiming batches. Export myplace-ready bulk payment requests (BPR), and see at a glance what is generated, partially paid, paid and outstanding.

STAY AUDIT-READY
Track NDIS Worker Checks, certificates, training and expiry dates. Keep price caps, pay rates and compliance records current. Tenant-scoped data access, Firebase authentication, App Check attestation and server-verified role checks keep claiming and financial configuration in administrator hands.

KEEP FAMILIES CLOSE
A dedicated client portal shows appointments, service history and invoices. Participants and families can view, approve or dispute an invoice, message their team and set granular family-access permissions — without ever seeing another participant's data.

BUILT FOR REAL SHIFTS
An offline queue syncs when connectivity returns, push notifications keep everyone informed, and a hands-free voice assistant handles common navigation.

WHO IT IS FOR
NDIS service providers · disability support workers · support coordinators · plan managers · allied health professionals · care organisation administrators

CareNest replaces the whiteboard, the inbox and the spreadsheet with one reliable system — so your team spends its time on care, not administration.
