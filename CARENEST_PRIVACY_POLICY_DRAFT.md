# CareNest Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 25, 2026

## Overview

CareNest provides care operations tools for providers, workers, clients, and authorized family members. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how long we retain it, and how users can request account deletion.

## Information We Collect

Depending on how you use CareNest, we may collect:

- Account information, such as name, email address, phone number, role, organization, and login details
- Profile information, such as profile photos and organization details
- Care and service information, such as appointments, attendance events, service confirmations, notes, forms, invoices, receipts, and related documents
- Communication data, such as secure messages and support requests
- Device and usage data, such as app diagnostics, crash information, notification tokens, and basic usage events
- Location data, including background location during scheduled visits when required for attendance, arrival reminders, navigation, and care-service workflows
- Media and voice inputs, such as uploaded photos, scanned documents, microphone recordings, and speech-to-text input when those features are used

## How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

- Create and manage user accounts
- Authenticate users and protect account security
- Deliver scheduling, attendance, invoicing, messaging, and care-service workflows
- Send notifications about appointments, invoices, reminders, and service activity
- Support compliance, recordkeeping, payroll, tax, audit, and care-service obligations
- Improve app reliability, troubleshoot issues, and respond to support requests

## Data Sharing

We may share information with service providers and infrastructure partners that help us operate CareNest, including authentication, messaging, file storage, crash reporting, analytics, and hosting providers. We may also disclose information when required to comply with law, regulation, audit, payroll, tax, safeguarding, or legal process obligations.

CareNest does not sell personal information.

## Account Deletion And Data Retention

Users can initiate account deletion from within the app.

When a deletion request is approved:

- the account is deactivated
- access to the account is removed
- permanent deletion is scheduled for up to 90 days later

During that retention period, CareNest may retain only the records required for payroll, tax, audit, safeguarding, accounting, legal, and care-service obligations. This retention is for compliance and recordkeeping purposes, not for convenience or marketing reasons.

After the applicable retention period ends, remaining personal data that is no longer required will be deleted or anonymized.

Some records may need to be retained longer if required by applicable law, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, or regulatory obligations.

## Your Choices And Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to:

- access your personal data
- correct inaccurate information
- request deletion of your account and eligible data
- withdraw consent for optional permissions such as camera, microphone, speech recognition, notifications, or location access

You can also manage certain device permissions in your iPhone settings.

## Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children

CareNest is not intended for use by children without appropriate authorization and supervision.

## Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date above.
