- messaging or client portal issues
- app crashes, bugs, and unexpected behavior
- account deletion and privacy questions

## Account Deletion

Users can initiate account deletion from within the app.

After approval of a deletion request:

- the account is deactivated
- access is removed
- permanent deletion is scheduled for up to 90 days later

During that retention window, CareNest may retain only the records required for payroll, tax, audit, safeguarding, accounting, legal, and care-service obligations.

If you need help with an existing deletion request, contact support and include the email address associated with your account.
